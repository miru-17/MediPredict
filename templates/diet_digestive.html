Digestive Health Dietary Plan

🌱 Core Principles of a Digestive-Healthy Diet

  • Gentle, easily digestible foods to reduce strain on stomach and intestines
  • Balanced fiber intake (soluble + controlled insoluble fiber)
  • Probiotics & prebiotics for gut microbiome balance
  • Hydration (8–10 glasses + herbal teas)
  • Small, frequent meals to prevent bloating

✅ Foods to Include

  • Cereals & Grains: Brown rice, oats, ragi, quinoa, upma, khichdi
  • Fruits: Apples, bananas, papaya, watermelon, pears, pomegranate
  • Vegetables: Pumpkin, bottle gourd, carrots, cabbage, zucchini
  • Pulses: Moong dal, masoor dal (easy to digest)
  • Dairy: Curd, buttermilk, low-fat yogurt
  • Spices: Cumin, turmeric, ginger, fennel, cinnamon
  • Healthy Drinks: Coconut water, soups, herbal teas

❌ Foods to Avoid

  • Refined flour (white bread, pastries)
  • Spicy and fried foods
  • Carbonated drinks and alcohol
  • Red meat and processed foods
  • Canned foods and artificial sweeteners

🍽️ Sample Daily Diet Plan

  • Early Morning: Warm water with fennel or ginger tea
  • Breakfast: Upma / poha / oats with banana
  • Mid-Morning: Coconut water or papaya
  • Lunch: Chapati/brown rice + dal + pumpkin curry
  • Evening: Soup or herbal tea
  • Dinner: Khichdi or ragi + vegetables + dal
  • Bedtime: Warm water with fennel

⚠️ Practical Tips

  • Chew food thoroughly
  • Avoid skipping meals
  • Walk daily (30–45 mins)
  • Prefer whole fruits over juices
  • Manage stress
