Heart-Healthy Dietary Plan

❤️ Core Dietary Principles

  • Increase Fiber: Whole-wheat bread, brown rice, quinoa, oatmeal, beans, lentils
  • Healthy Fats: Olive oil, canola oil, nuts, avocados (avoid butter & coconut oil)
  • Lean Proteins: Fish, poultry, plant-based proteins (limit red meat)
  • Reduce Sodium & Sugar: Less than 1500–2300 mg sodium daily
  • Increase Fruits & Vegetables: Leafy greens and berries

🍽️ Sample Meal Ideas

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with flaxseeds & berries OR egg white scramble
  • Lunch: Salad with vegetables, beans, chicken/salmon & olive oil dressing
  • Dinner: Baked fish with brown rice and steamed broccoli

🥜 Healthy Snacks

  • Fruits & Vegetables:
    Apples, pears, grapes, papaya, citrus fruits – high in fiber and antioxidants.
    Carrot, cucumber, bell pepper, broccoli, cauliflower – crunchy, low-calorie, nutrient-dense.
    Beets & cruciferous veggies (broccoli, cabbage, kale) – support blood pressure regulation.
  • Nuts & Seeds:
    Walnuts, almonds, flaxseeds, chia seeds – rich in omega-3 fats, magnesium, and fiber.
    Trail mix with walnuts & tart cherries – combines anti-inflammatory antioxidants with healthy fats.
  • Protein-Rich Snacks:
    Boiled eggs (moderation) – provide protein and essential nutrients.
    Roasted chickpeas or hummus with veggies – plant-based protein and fiber.
    Fatty fish (sardines, salmon, tuna) – excellent source of omega-3 fatty acids.
  • Whole Grains:
    Air-popped popcorn – whole grain, low-calorie, heart-friendly when unsalted.
    Whole-grain crackers with low-sodium tuna or salmon – combines fiber with lean protein.
  • Dairy & Alternatives:
    Low-fat yogurt with fruit – probiotics plus antioxidants.
    Fat-free cottage cheese with pear/apple slices – protein-rich, low in saturated fat.
  • Drinks:
    Matcha green tea – rich in EGCG, reduces inflammation and arterial plaque.
    Unsweetened tea/coffee, sparkling water with citrus – hydrating without added sugars.

🚫 Foods to Avoid / Limit

  • Trans fats: Processed snacks, hydrogenated oils
  • Saturated fats: Butter, cheese, fatty meat, fast food
  • Refined carbs: White bread, pasta, pastries
  • Sugary drinks: Soda, sweetened beverages
