Neurological Health Dietary Plan

🌱 Neurological Diet Plan

  • Omega-3 fatty acids: Found in fish, walnuts, flaxseeds, chia seeds – support neuron function
  • Antioxidants: Berries, spinach, kale, turmeric, dark chocolate – protect brain cells
  • B vitamins: Whole grains, legumes, eggs – support neurotransmitters
  • Vitamin D: Sunlight, dairy, fish – improves mood and reduces inflammation
  • Magnesium: Nuts, seeds, greens – calms nervous system
  • Probiotics: Yogurt, kefir, kimchi – support gut-brain health

🍽️ Daily Eating Pattern

  • Early Morning: Warm water or herbal tea + soaked almonds/walnuts
  • Breakfast: Oats / ragi dosa / upma + egg or sprouts
  • Mid-Morning: Fruits (berries, apple, papaya) + seeds
  • Lunch: Chapati/brown rice + dal + spinach + fish/chicken
  • Afternoon Snack: Yogurt + flaxseeds or vegetable soup
  • Dinner: Ragi/quinoa + vegetables + salad
  • Hydration: 8–10 glasses water + herbal teas

❌ Foods to Limit or Avoid

  • Sugary foods and drinks
  • Processed foods (snacks, instant foods)
  • Trans fats and saturated fats
  • Excess salt

⚠️ Practical Lifestyle Tips

  • Eat smaller balanced meals
  • Prefer steaming, grilling, baking
  • Exercise, yoga, meditation
  • Maintain proper sleep routine
