Respiratory Health Dietary Plan

🌿 Core Dietary Principles

  • High Protein: Maintain muscle strength (including diaphragm muscles)
  • Anti-inflammatory Foods: Rich in Vitamin C, E, A (beta-carotene)
  • Healthy Fats (Omega-3): Reduce airway inflammation
  • High Fiber: Supports better lung function
  • Hydration: 6–8 glasses of fluids daily

✅ Foods to Include Daily

  • Fruits & Vegetables:
    Vitamin C-rich (citrus, bell peppers, berries) – boost immunity and lung function.
    Leafy greens (spinach, kale) – antioxidants and magnesium.
    Cruciferous veggies (broccoli, cauliflower) – anti-inflammatory.
  • Whole Grains:
    Brown rice, oats, quinoa – fiber and steady energy.
  • Protein Sources:
    Fish (salmon, sardines, tuna) – omega-3 reduces inflammation.
    Poultry, eggs, beans, lentils – maintain muscle strength.
    Low-fat dairy or alternatives – calcium and protein.
  • Healthy Fats:
    Nuts, seeds, olive oil, flaxseed – support lung cell health.
  • Hydration:
    Water, herbal teas – thin mucus and ease coughing.
    Limit carbonated drinks.

🚫 Foods to Avoid / Limit

  • Processed meats (bacon, sausage)
  • Fried and fatty foods
  • Excess salt
  • Sugary drinks and alcohol
  • Gas-producing foods (cabbage, beans, soda)

📅 Sample Daily Meal Plan

  • Early Morning: Lemon water or herbal tea
  • Breakfast: Vegetable poha / upma / oatmeal
  • Mid-Morning: Nuts or berries
  • Lunch: Brown rice/roti + dal + vegetables + curd
  • Evening: Green tea or vegetable soup
  • Dinner: Grilled fish/chicken + vegetables

💡 Additional Lifestyle Tips

  • Eat small frequent meals (5–6 times)
  • Eat slowly and sit upright
  • Maintain healthy weight to reduce lung strain
