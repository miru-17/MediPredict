Skin Health Dietary Plan

🧴 Key Dietary Principles

  • Anti-Inflammatory Foods: Focus on colorful fruits and vegetables (berries, leafy greens, carrots)
  • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Crucial for reducing inflammation and maintaining the skin’s lipid barrier
  • Probiotics & Prebiotics: Support a healthy gut-skin axis, reducing inflammation (eczema, psoriasis)
  • Hydration: Drink 7–8 glasses of water daily

🥗 Foods to Include

  • Fatty Fish: Salmon, mackerel, sardines, herring
  • Nuts & Seeds: Walnuts, flaxseeds, chia seeds, sunflower seeds
  • Berries & Fruits: Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries, papaya
  • Leafy Greens & Vegetables: Spinach, kale, broccoli, carrots, sweet potatoes
  • Healthy Fats: Olive oil, avocados
  • Probiotic Foods: Yogurt, kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi
  • Spices: Turmeric, ginger

🚫 Foods to Avoid / Limit

  • Refined carbs & sugars (white bread, pasta, soda, candies)
  • Dairy products (especially skim milk)
  • Processed & fried foods (fast food, chips, processed meats)
  • Alcohol
  • Trigger foods: Gluten, soy, eggs, peanuts
  • High nickel foods: Whole grains, nuts, chocolate

📅 Sample Daily Diet Plan

  • Early Morning: Warm water with lemon OR Amla juice
  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with berries OR spinach omelet
  • Mid-Morning: Walnuts or fruit (apple/pear)
  • Lunch: Grilled salmon with vegetables OR salad
  • Evening: Green tea with almonds
  • Dinner: Lentil soup OR rice with tofu & vegetables
