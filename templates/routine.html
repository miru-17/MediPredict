Healthy Daily Routine

🌅 Morning

06:00 - 06:30 AM
💧 Wake up at the same time, drink 1–2 glasses of water (optionally with lemon), and get direct morning sunlight to regulate your circadian rhythm.
06:30 - 07:15 AM
🧘 Exercise (yoga, brisk walk, or HIIT), or practice 10-20 minutes of meditation for stress reduction.
07:30 - 08:00 AM
🍳 High-protein, healthy breakfast (e.g., oats, eggs, fruit).
08:00 AM
🪥 Daily hygiene (brushing/flossing)

☀️ Afternoon

10:30 AM
🥜 Short, healthy snacks (e.g., nuts, fruits).
12:30 - 01:30 PM
🍽️ Lunch: Balanced meal with lean protein, fiber-rich vegetables, and whole grains.
01:30 - 02:00 PM
🚶 Short walk or "mini-movement breaks" every hour to reduce sedentary time.
03:30 PM
🍎 Short, healthy snacks (e.g., nuts, fruits).
Throughout
💦 Keep a water bottle handy, drinking at regular intervals.

🌙 Evening

07:00 - 08:00 PM
🥗 Light dinner (try to finish at least 2-3 hours before bed).
08:00 - 09:30 PM
📖 Unwind: Screen-free time (limit social media/phones), reading, or socializing with family
09:30 - 10:00 PM
🌙 Prepare for sleep (cool, dark, quiet room).
