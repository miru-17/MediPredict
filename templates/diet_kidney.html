Kidney-Healthy Dietary Plan

🌱 Core Principles of a Kidney-Healthy Diet

  • Moderate protein intake to avoid kidney strain
  • Low sodium to control blood pressure and fluid retention
  • Controlled potassium and phosphorus levels
  • Adequate calories to prevent fatigue and muscle loss
  • Balanced hydration based on medical advice

✅ Foods to Include

  • Fruits (low potassium): Apples, grapes, berries, pineapple, watermelon
  • Vegetables (low potassium): Cabbage, cauliflower, cucumber, lettuce, bell peppers
  • Grains: White rice, chapati, oats, semolina, broken wheat
  • Protein (controlled): Egg whites, chicken, fish, moong dal
  • Healthy fats: Olive oil, flaxseed oil, limited nuts
  • Dairy (limited): Low-fat milk or curd (small amounts)
  • Hydration: Herbal teas and water

❌ Foods to Avoid

  • High sodium foods (pickles, chips, processed foods)
  • High potassium foods (banana, potato, tomato, spinach)
  • High phosphorus foods (nuts, chocolate, cola)
  • Red meat and organ meats
  • Packaged and processed foods

🍽️ Sample Daily Diet Plan

  • Morning: Warm water + apple slices
  • Breakfast: Oats with berries or vegetable upma
  • Mid-Morning: Grapes or cucumber sticks
  • Lunch: Rice/chapati + moong dal + cabbage curry + salad
  • Afternoon Snack: Herbal tea + puffed rice or makhana
  • Dinner: Chapati + cauliflower curry + small curd
  • Bedtime: Warm water or fennel infusion

⚠️ Practical Tips

  • Double boil vegetables to reduce potassium
  • Use herbs instead of salt
  • Monitor lab values regularly
  • Balance protein intake carefully
  • Follow fluid restrictions if advised
