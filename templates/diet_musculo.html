Musculoskeletal Health Dietary Plan

🌱 Core Nutrients for Musculoskeletal Health

  • Omega-3 fatty acids: Reduce inflammation (fish, walnuts, flaxseeds, chia seeds)
  • Calcium & Vitamin D: Strengthen bones (dairy, leafy greens, sunlight)
  • Magnesium & Zinc: Support muscle and bone repair (seeds, lentils, grains)
  • Protein: Builds and repairs muscle tissue (poultry, eggs, beans, yogurt)
  • Antioxidants: Reduce oxidative stress (berries, citrus, turmeric, green tea)

✅ Recommended Foods

  • Fruits & Vegetables: Berries, cherries, grapes, avocados, spinach, broccoli, carrots
  • Healthy Fats: Olive oil, nuts, seeds, fatty fish
  • Whole Grains: Brown rice, oats, quinoa, whole wheat chapati
  • Proteins: Cottage cheese, lentils, beans, chicken, salmon, tuna
  • Hydrating Foods: Cucumber, watermelon, soups, herbal teas

❌ Foods to Avoid

  • Processed foods (snacks, fried items)
  • Excess salt
  • Sugary foods and drinks
  • Excess red meat

🍽️ Sample Daily Diet Plan

  • Morning: Herbal tea + soaked almonds/walnuts
  • Breakfast: Oats with berries OR ragi dosa
  • Mid-Morning: Fruit + pumpkin seeds
  • Lunch: Brown rice/chapati + dal + spinach + fish/chicken
  • Afternoon Snack: Yogurt + flaxseeds or soup
  • Dinner: Quinoa/ragi + vegetables + salad
  • Hydration: 8–10 glasses water + green tea

⚠️ Practical Tips

  • Maintain healthy weight
  • Prefer steaming, grilling, baking
  • Exercise and strength training
  • Ensure sunlight exposure for Vitamin D
